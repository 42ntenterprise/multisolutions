Process

How it works

Each stage shows what happens, what you receive, how long it takes, and what we need from your side.

1
Tell us what you need to solve (today or in a short call)

You share your goal, urgency, and how you currently work with your data so we can understand whether it makes sense to move forward and where to begin.

By the end you will have

Initial clarity about your case and whether it makes sense to move forward.

Timing

Today or in a short call.

What we need from you

Main objective, current sources, and urgency.

2
You receive an initial assessment and next step (within 24 business hours)

We review your context and respond with concrete opportunities, a recommended approach, and, if applicable, a demo or visual proposal.

By the end you will have

An initial review, priorities, and a recommended next step.

Timing

Up to 24 business hours.

What we need from you

Only the basic information from step 1.

3
We implement with visible progress (2 to 8 weeks, depending on complexity)

We execute in phases with validations and partial results so you can see functional progress before final handoff.

By the end you will have

Visible functional progress, partial deliverables, and control over progress.

Timing

Between 2 and 8 weeks, depending on complexity and validations.

What we need from you

Data access, KPI validation, and feedback at key milestones.

4
You receive everything ready and your team remains autonomous (formal project close)

We close with handoff, transfer, and optional support so you can maintain continuity without future dependency.

By the end you will have

A dashboard, documentation, and continuity without future dependency.

Timing

Formal close according to the validated scope.

What we need from you

Final review, approval, and handoff coordination.
