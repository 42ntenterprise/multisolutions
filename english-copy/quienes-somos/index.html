About us

42NT Solutions was born in Chile with one conviction: strategic decisions cannot depend on confusing or fragmented information.

Our vision is practical and demanding. We believe technology should serve the business, not impose itself on it. That is why, before talking about tools, we focus on understanding how each client thinks, operates, and decides. Only then do we design useful solutions that support long-term adoption.

We build relationships based on judgment, technical solidity, and trust. We turn complexity into order and visibility, giving leaders and teams the exact tools they need to reach their goals.

Today we are extending that standard into new markets. We want to support more organizations through serious, modern business intelligence: close in collaboration, rigorous in execution, and focused on results.

What we do

We design integrated, measurable, and easy-to-use solutions so teams can decide with less manual work and more clarity. We connect sources, define KPIs, prepare the data foundation, and deliver dashboards ready to operate. We can start from Excel, APIs, or other messy sources, always with clear scope, visible progress, and full handoff to the client. We do not sell isolated screens: we leave behind a reliable foundation, documentation, automations, and operational autonomy, with optional support according to each need.

We work with direct technical leadership and agile methods so every delivery is useful, understandable, and sustainable over time. We do not sell isolated dashboards: we build a reliable base so your organization can manage information better and make decisions with more confidence, discipline, and solidity.
